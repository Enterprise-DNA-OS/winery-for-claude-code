# Winery record checks

Checked 1 October 2026. These checks find gaps in recorded evidence. They do not certify a winery, approve wine, assess chemical treatment, file returns, or replace a registered wine standards management plan. AU and NZ rules apply only to owners marked with that jurisdiction. Confirm the owner's legal role and export markets before deployment.

| Check | Recorded evidence checked | Source and scope |
|---|---|---|
| AU-LIP-3D | Event date versus record creation date. Flags more than three calendar days. | [Wine Australia producer audit guidance](https://www.wineaustralia.com/news/articles/what-are-we-looking-for-during-an-audit), transaction records within three days. |
| AU-LIP-7Y | Operation retention date covers at least seven years from event date. | [Label Integrity Program](https://www.wineaustralia.com/labelling/label-integrity-program), retain LIP records seven years. The program never deletes history automatically. Backups and access remain the operator's job. |
| AU-LIP-SUPPLY | Recipient address, supply statement reference and seven-year retention. | [Label Integrity Program](https://www.wineaustralia.com/labelling/label-integrity-program). Generated drafts include supplier, recipient, quantity, date, record maker and origin composition. A reference proves a reference was entered, not that a statement was delivered. |
| TRACE-ORIGIN | Vineyard, vintage, variety and region exist for each lot. | [Wine Australia](https://www.wineaustralia.com/labelling/label-integrity-program) requires an auditable origin trail. [MPI verifier guidance](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/food-business/winemaking-standards-requirements-and-testing/wine-standards-management-plans/verification-for-winemaking/working-as-a-wine-verifier) includes traceability and winemaking inputs. This check tests completeness, not label eligibility or percentage claims. |
| NZ-RECALL-12M | A recall exercise record exists for that NZ owner in the preceding twelve months. | [MPI simulated recall guidance](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/food-business/food-recalls/doing-food-recall). Applicable businesses must practise recall at least every twelve months, with qualifying real recalls able to count. Enter a real exercise, reviewer and evidence, never a fictional pass. |
| POLICY-HOLD | Wine on hold is identified and movement commands reject it. | Local operating policy, not legislation. Release requires the recorded operator and reason. |
| POLICY-LAB-14D | No sample or latest sample more than fourteen days old. | Demonstration policy only. It is not a legal limit, a complete lab panel check or a statement that wine is safe. A winemaker sets metric-specific intervals and release specifications for their operation. |

Receive records capture supplier address and origin. Blends retain weighted origin records and parent links. Trace follows a source lot into descendant blends, current positions, bulk supplies and bottlings. Additions preserve supplier and material batch. Bottling records identify packaging references. No automatic label claims, excise, export certificates, allergen decisions or regulatory submissions are included.

For a live winery, reconcile historical movements and origin evidence, configure roles and backups, and validate the full WSMP with the verifier. This database is a record-keeping base for that work, not a regulator-approved system.
